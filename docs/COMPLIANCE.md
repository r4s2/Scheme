# Publisher rules in the engine

`src/engine/compliance.ts` enforces these when an event is created or its settings change. Rules change; review them each quarter.

| Rule | Applies to | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At least 70% of entry fees go to the prize pool | League, Valorant | [Riot developer docs](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol), [Valorant API policy](https://support-developer.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/22698769097107-VALORANT) |
| At least 20 participants for paid events | League, Valorant | [Riot developer docs](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol) |
| Register events through Riot's visibility form | League, Valorant | [Esports News UK, Aug 2026](https://esports-news.co.uk/2026/08/04/league-of-legends-new-community-tournament-guidelines-triumphant-ryze/) |
| Prizes at least equal to entry fees collected | Overwatch | [Overwatch community license](https://esports.overwatch.com/en-us/third-party-license) |
| No "presented by" or title sponsor in the name | Overwatch | [Overwatch community license](https://esports.overwatch.com/en-us/third-party-license) |
| Names can't use Marvel, Ignite, Final, NetEase, Clash, Official, Cup | Marvel Rivals | [Marvel Rivals third-party guideline](https://www.marvelrivalsesports.com/20260603/42831_1302721.html) |
| Required NetEase disclaimer on event materials | Marvel Rivals | [Marvel Rivals third-party guideline](https://www.marvelrivalsesports.com/20260603/42831_1302721.html) |
| No publisher trademarks in event names | All | General trademark practice |

Scheme's own rules, also enforced there: paid payout splits must total 100%, paid events need host karma of 75 or more, and the rank floor can't exceed the ceiling.
